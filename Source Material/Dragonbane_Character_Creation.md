# Dragonbane Character Creation

Character creation rules extracted from the Dragonbane Rules Reference, with the name, nickname, weakness, memento and appearance tables from the *Dragonbane Rulebook* added. Page references point to the printed rulebook pages.

*Rulebook pp. 9-29*

## Creation sequence

1. Choose or roll kin and note its innate ability.
2. Choose or roll profession.
3. Choose age.
4. Choose or roll a name, and optionally a nickname.
5. Roll and assign attributes.
6. Calculate derived ratings.
7. Select trained skills.
8. Note the profession's heroic ability, or magic for a mage.
9. Optionally choose a weakness and memento.
10. Select a profession gear package.
11. Choose or roll appearance.

All player characters speak, read and write the common tongue. Non-human characters also know their own language. Use LANGUAGES for other languages.

## Attributes

The six attributes are STR, CON, AGL, INT, WIL and CHA. For each attribute, roll 4D6 and discard the lowest die. Assign each result as it is rolled; after all six have been assigned, swap any two if desired. Scores normally range from 3 to 18.

| Attribute score | Base chance for linked skills |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1-5 | 3 |
| 6-8 | 4 |
| 9-12 | 5 |
| 13-15 | 6 |
| 16-18 | 7 |

## Derived ratings

- **HP maximum:** CON; Robust can increase it.
- **WP maximum:** WIL; Focused can increase it.
- **Movement:** kin's base, modified by AGL:

| AGL | Movement modifier |
| ---: | ---: |
| 1-6 | -4 |
| 7-9 | -2 |
| 10-12 | - |
| 13-15 | +2 |
| 16-18 | +4 |

- **Damage bonus:** calculate separately for STR-based and AGL-based weapons.

| Relevant attribute | Damage bonus |
| ---: | --- |
| 12 or lower | None |
| 13-16 | +D4 |
| 17 or higher | +D6 |

## Starting skills

There are 30 core skills. An untrained core skill begins at the base chance of its linked attribute. A trained starting skill begins at **twice** its base chance. Six trained skills must come from the profession list; the remaining two, four or six are free choices according to age.

Schools of magic are secondary skills. They have no base chance unless learned. A new mage selects one school as a trained, INT-based skill.

## Starting abilities and magic

- A non-mage begins with the heroic ability granted by their profession. A starting ability ignores its normal skill prerequisite.
- A mage begins with no heroic ability, but learns one school, three rank 1 spells and three magic tricks from that school or General Magic.

## Gear and money

- Record worn armour, helmet and up to three ready weapons in their dedicated areas; they do not count towards Inventory encumbrance.
- All other carried items must be recorded in Inventory.
- Every character also has simple clothes.
- Currency: 10 copper = 1 silver; 10 silver = 1 gold.

## Encumbrance

- **Carrying capacity:** half STR, rounded up, in Inventory weight units.
- **Ready weapons:** up to three weapons at hand; shields count as weapons. These do not count towards carrying capacity.
- **Worn armour and helmet:** do not count towards carrying capacity.
- **Food:** up to four rations count as weight 1.
- **Arrows:** a quiver counts as weight 1; individual arrows are not tracked. Slingstones are not tracked.
- **Backpack:** increases capacity by 2, has no weight itself, and only one may be used.
- **Heavy items:** use as many Inventory rows as their listed weight.
- **Tiny items:** no encumbrance. Fewer than 100 coins are tiny; 100-199 coins weigh 1, 200-299 weigh 2, and so forth.
- **Over-encumbered:** roll STR whenever attempting to move in a combat round or travel for a shift. On failure, drop enough items or remain in place.
- **Carrying a person:** always over-encumbered and unable to fight.

## Experience and advancement

Mark a skill when it rolls a Dragon or Demon. At session end, add one mark to a currently unmarked skill for every justified **yes**:

- Did you participate in the session?
- Did you explore a new location?
- Did you defeat one or more dangerous adversaries?
- Did you overcome an obstacle without force?
- Did you give in to your weakness? `[Optional]`

For each marked skill, roll D20. If the result exceeds its current level, increase the skill by 1, to a maximum of 18. Erase all marks afterwards.

**Teacher:** one shift with a teacher whose level is at least 15 and higher than yours grants an immediate extra advancement roll. A teacher can raise the skill only once before you must improve it through experience.

**New heroic abilities:** gain one when a skill reaches 18, or as a rare reward for a grand heroic deed. You must meet the new ability's prerequisite. If a skill later drops below 18, keep the ability; raising it to 18 again grants another.
