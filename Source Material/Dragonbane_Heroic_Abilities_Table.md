# Dragonbane: Heroic Abilities

## Heroic Abilities

| Ability | Prerequisite | WP | Effect |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Assassin | Knives 12 | 3 | A sneak attack deals +D8 damage. May combine with Backstabbing. Activate after the hit roll but before damage. |
| Backstabbing | Knives 12 | 3 | With a subtle melee weapon, attack an enemy within 2 m of another player character. It becomes a sneak attack: boon, cannot be dodged or parried, and +1 weapon damage die. No action to activate. |
| Battle Cry | None | 3 | As an action in combat, all other player characters within earshot heal one condition of their choice. |
| Berserker | Any melee weapon skill 12 | 3 | Become Angry, or take another condition if already Angry; immediately attack the closest melee opponent. Continue until every visible opponent is defeated or you reach 0 HP. Gain a boon to melee attacks but cannot dodge or parry. Become Exhausted after the fight. |
| Catlike | Acrobatics 12 | Varies | Reduce falling damage by one D6 per WP spent. May make the ACROBATICS roll first. |
| Companion | Hunting and Fishing 12 | 3 | In one stretch, make one nearby non-monster animal your companion. It follows while in its natural environment and scouts without more WP. Spend 3 further WP to command an attack; this is a free action for you. Only one companion at a time. |
| Contortionist | Evade 12 | 1 | Escape restraints or squeeze through a narrow space without a roll. |
| Defensive | Any melee weapon skill 12 | 3 | Make one parry without spending your round's action. May be used repeatedly with enough WP, but only one parry attempt per attack and never both dodge and parry the same attack. |
| Deflect Arrow | Any melee weapon skill 12 | 1 | Parry a ranged attack with a melee weapon instead of a shield. |
| Disguise | Bluffing 12 | 2 | After one stretch, copy the appearance, voice and manner of a same-kin person. Someone who knows them and sees you within 10 m may roll AWARENESS to detect the disguise. |
| Double Slash | Axes or Swords 12 | 3 | With a slashing weapon, make one attack roll against two enemies within 2 m. Each defends separately; roll damage separately. May combine with Dual Wield. |
| Dragonslayer | Any weapon skill 12 | 3 | Deal +D8 damage to a monster. Activate after hitting but before damage. |
| Dual Wield | Any melee weapon skill 12 | 3 | Requires a one-handed weapon in each hand; off-hand STR requirement is +3. Gain an extra attack with the second weapon at a bane. Resolve the attacks in either order, one completely before the other. May combine with Double Slash. |
| Eagle Eye | Awareness 12 | 2 | For one stretch, view a target up to 200 m away as if adjacent. In combat, ignore the bane for shooting beyond effective range; activate separately for each target. |
| Fast Footwork | Evade 12 | 3 | Make one dodge without spending your round's action. Repeatable with enough WP, but only one dodge attempt per attack and never both dodge and parry the same attack. |
| Fast Healer | None | 2 | Heal +D6 HP during a stretch rest; no extra WP or conditions. |
| Fearless | None | 2 | Automatically resist a fear attack without rolling WIL. |
| Focused | None | - | Permanently increase maximum WP by 2. May be selected repeatedly. |
| Guardian | Axes, Hammers or Swords 12 | 2 | If you and an ally are both within 2 m of an enemy that attacks the ally, force the enemy to attack you instead. Out of turn; no action. |
| Insight | Persuasion 12 | 2 | After speaking with someone for a while, roll AWARENESS to determine whether they are telling the truth, but not which statement is false. |
| Intuition | Myths and Legends 12 | 3 | At a difficult decision, ask the GM one question and receive a helpful answer representing broad knowledge; it aids the decision but does not reveal everything. |
| Iron Fist | Brawling 12 | 1 | Add one D6 to an unarmed attack's damage. Free activation after the attack roll. |
| Iron Grip | Brawling 12 | 1 | Gain a boon to BRAWLING when grappling or resisting escape from your grapple. |
| Lightning Fast | Evade 12 | 2 | At the start of a round, draw two initiative cards and keep one. Once per round. |
| Lone Wolf | Bushcraft 12 | - | Take a shift rest in the wilderness without a BUSHCRAFT camp roll. Applies only to you, even when using a tent. |
| Magic Talent | None | - | Gain access to learning one new school of magic. Spells are learned separately. May be selected once per new school. |
| Massive Blow | Any STR-based melee skill 12 | 3 | A two-handed melee hit deals +D8 damage, but only if you did not move and will not move that round. Activate after the hit roll. |
| Master Blacksmith | Crafting 12 | Varies | With blacksmithing tools, sharpen a blade or point in one stretch for 3 WP; armour counts one step lower against it until it is used in one combat encounter to attack or parry. In one shift, forge a listed metal weapon or armour using a forge, anvil and iron (weight 1); WP cost equals listed gold price rounded up and work may span shifts. |
| Master Carpenter | Crafting 12 | Varies | With carpentry tools, deal D12 damage per WP to an inanimate object as an action, ignoring armour. In one shift, craft a wooden listed item from suitable wood; WP equals gold price rounded up. GM prices unlisted items. |
| Master Chef | None | 1 | Automatically cook food successfully without a BUSHCRAFT roll. |
| Master Spellcaster | Any school of magic 12 | 3 | On your turn, cast two different spells as one action. You may roll the first spell before activating. |
| Master Tanner | Crafting 12 | Varies | With tanning tools, make leather armour from an animal or monster hide in one shift. Armour rating is half the creature's armour, rounded up, minimum 1; WP cost equals the resulting rating. |
| Monster Hunter | Beast Lore 12 | 3 | At a crossroads, learn the direction of the most dangerous enemies. |
| Musician | Performance 12 | 3 | As an action, either give all allies within 10 m a boon to all rolls or all enemies in range a bane to all rolls, until your turn next round. Instruments can alter cost or range. |
| Pathfinder | Bushcraft 12 | 1 | Gain a boon to BUSHCRAFT when finding direction in the wilderness. |
| Quartermaster | Bushcraft 12 | 1 | Automatically succeed when making camp during a journey. |
| Robust | None | - | Permanently increase maximum HP by 2. May be selected repeatedly. |
| Sea Legs | Swimming 12 | 1 | For one round, ignore all negative effects of acting in water, even waist-deep, including drowning risk. No action. |
| Shield Block | Any STR-based melee skill 12 | 2 | Gain a boon when parrying with a shield. Also permits a shield parry, with a boon, against physical monster attacks that are not area effects. May combine with Defensive. |
| Throwing Arm | Any melee weapon skill 12 | 2 | Throw a one-handed melee weapon at a target within STR metres. Roll the normal weapon skill; the target may defend normally. Weapon lands at the target's feet. |
| Treasure Hunter | Bartering 12 | 3 | At a crossroads, learn the direction of the greatest treasure. |
| Twin Shot | Bows 12 | 3 | With a bow, not a crossbow, fire two arrows using one attack roll with a bane. Aim at one or two targets and roll damage separately. |
| Veteran | Any weapon skill 12 | 1 | At a round's start, retain the previous round's initiative card instead of drawing. No action. |
| Weasel | Evade 12 | 3 | Before defending against a non-area attack, redirect it to another player character within 2 m. The new target may dodge or parry normally. |
