## Schools of Magic

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| General Magic | Not a skill of its own: any mage with a magic-school skill can learn these spells (harmonists cannot). Covers universal utility magic such as alarms, dispelling, protective wards, familiars, magic seals, charging items and permanent rituals. |
| Animism | Nature magic of healing, plants, animals and weather, from treating wounds and calming beasts to ensnaring roots, lightning and resurrection. Higher ranks require expertise in Beast Lore, Bushcraft or Seamanship. |
| Demonology | Draws on Chaos and demons: foul stenches, beetle swarms, rage, dimensional travel and summoned demon champions and guardians. Advanced spells are learned through cults or risky demonic pacts. |
| Elementalism | Command of fire, water, air and earth: fireballs, stone walls, tidal waves, storms and summoned elemental servants. Elementalists can bind familiars to elemental spirits instead of animals. |
| Harmonism | Musical magic cast with PERFORMANCE rather than a school skill, normally practised by bards. Many spells are activated by melody and sway moods, minds and bodies. Harmonists cannot learn General Magic. |
| Illusionism | Magic of deception and shadow: apparitions, disguises, false memories, mirror images, false terrain and manipulated dreams. |
| Mentalism | Mastery of mind over body and matter: levitation, enhanced strength and senses, telepathy, flight, teleportation and domination of people and monsters. |
| Necromancy | Magic of death and undeath: fear, pain, life-draining, speaking with the dead and raising carcasses, skeletons, wights and mummies. |
| Symbolism | Rune magic worked through symbols traced in the air, drawn or carved; the method sets casting time and duration. Spells include wards, barriers, magic armour, golems, portals and runic prisons. |
| Witchcraft | Magic of animals, prophecy and curses: animal senses, augury, hexes, shapeshifting, polymorph and control of monsters. Training is usually paid in service rather than coin. |
| Alchemy | A school whose spells are recipes: potions, poisons, bombs and elixirs brewed in a laboratory from ingredients, normally over one shift. Power level sets the number of doses. No magic tricks. |
| Enchanting | Binds spells directly into crafted or existing items such as weapons, armour and clothing; requires CRAFTING 12. Effects cost no WP to use, and drawbacks can add extra spells. No magic tricks. |
| Dracomancy | Rank 6 dragon magic that can only be learned during play by a mage who already knows a rank 5 spell. Costs 6 WP per power level and can reshape reality, freeze or reverse time and command dragons. |
