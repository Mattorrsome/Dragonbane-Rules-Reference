## Supply and Weapon Terms

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Normally available in any village or market. |
| Uncommon | Normally bought directly from a suitable artisan and may not be available in every village. |
| Rare | Generally must be specially ordered. |
| Unique | Normally found as treasure or obtained from its finder. |
| Grip | Hands needed to wield the weapon. |
| STR | Minimum Strength for effective use. |
| Range | Effective range in metres. |
| Damage | Dice rolled on a hit before damage bonus and armour. |
| Durability | Incoming damage a parrying item can withstand; if damage exceeds it, the item breaks until repaired. |

## Weapon Features

| Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Subtle | Grants the sneak-attack boon and additional damage die. |
| Long | Melee range 4 m and can attack past one friendly character. |
| Toppling | Grants a boon on the optional Topple attack. |
| Piercing, slashing, bludgeoning | Damage types. Declare piercing or slashing before attacking with a weapon that offers both. |
| Can be thrown | Use the normal weapon skill at the listed thrown range. |
